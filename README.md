 Disaster-Affected Family Reunification

This is a web application developed to help families and rescue teams find and share information about people who get separated during disasters such as floods, cyclones, and other emergencies.

During a disaster, a person may be taken to a shelter, hospital, evacuation centre, or another safe location. The family may not know where the person is, while different rescue teams may have information about the same person.

Our idea is to bring these details together in one place so that rescue teams can update information and families can track the status of their relatives.

What the system does

* Families can report a missing family member.
* Rescue teams can register people they find.
* Teams can update a person's location and status.
* The system can find possible matches between missing and found-person records.
* Authorized users can verify a match.
* Families can check the latest available status of their case.
* Reunification requests can be created after a match is verified.
* Volunteers can be registered and coordinated when additional support is needed.
